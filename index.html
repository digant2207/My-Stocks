          Detailed guide explaining how the composite score is evaluated and how Price Action logic determines Targets & Stop Loss levels.

          📊 How Company Composite Score (0 - 100) is Calculated

          1. Fundamental Health (35 Points)

          • Revenue Growth YoY (+10 pts for >15%, +6 pts for >5%).
          • Net Profit Growth YoY (+10 pts for >15%, +6 pts for >5%).
          • Return on Equity (ROE) (+10 pts for >18%, +7 pts for >12%).
          • Sector-Adjusted Debt Rule: Deducts up to 15 pts if Debt-to-Equity > 1.0 (Exempts Banks, NBFCs, Power & Infra).
          • Promoter Pledge Penalty: Deducts score if promoter pledged shares > 5%.

          2. Technical Oscillators & Trend (35 Points)

          • Moving Average Alignment (+8 pts above 20 EMA, +8 pts above 50 EMA, +7 pts above 200 EMA).
          • RSI Momentum (14) (+6 pts for sweet spot 50–68 RSI).
          • MACD Expansion (+6 pts for positive histogram expansion).

          3. Chart Pattern & Volume Surge (15 Points)

          • Classic Chart Patterns (+10 pts for Cup & Handle, Ascending Triangle, Bull Flag, VCP, W-Pattern).
          • Multi-Horizon Breakouts (+6 pts for 20D/50D Box Breakout, +8 pts for 52W High Breakout).
          • Volume Accumulation (RVOL) (+6 pts if Volume > 1.5x of 1-Month Avg Volume).

          4. Valuation & Analyst Upside (15 Points)

          • Analyst Consensus Target (+8 pts for >20% upside potential).
          • P/E Valuation Ratio (+7 pts for reasonable P/E between 0 and 35).

          🎯 Price Action Logic for Targets & Stop Loss

          1. 52-Week High Breakout Logic

          When price breaks past or trades near 52-Week High:

          • Target 1 (1-7D): 52W High + 1.5× ATR Volatility Expansion.
          • Target 2 (7-15D): 52W High + 3.2× ATR Volatility Expansion.
          • Stop Loss: Placed 1.5% below 20 DMA or 10-day swing bottom.

          2. 20-Day & 50-Day Range Box Breakout Logic

          When price closes above the 20-day or 50-day resistance ceiling:

          • Target 1 (1-7D): Breakout Price + Range Box Height (Measured Move).
          • Target 2 (7-15D): 52-Week High or 1.8x Range Box Height.
          • Stop Loss: Anchored 1% below Previous 20-day Swing Bottom.

          3. 20 DMA Breakout / Rebound Logic

          When price crosses above or bounces from 20-day Moving Average:

          • Target 1 (1-7D): Previous 20-day Swing High (Ceiling).
          • Target 2 (7-15D): 52-Week High level.
          • Stop Loss: Placed 1.5% below 20 DMA level.

          4. Previous Bottom Reversal (Double Bottom W-Pattern)

          When price bounces from support creating a double bottom W-reversal:

          • Target 1 & 2: W-Neckline + Depth of Double Bottom (Measured Move).
          • Stop Loss: Anchored right below the second bottom low pivot.
          Risk-to-Reward Ratio: Ensures every level calculation achieves a disciplined 1 : 2.2+ Risk-to-Reward ratio.
